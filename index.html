Lluís Torres-Hugas

Hi, I'm Lluís. I'm doing my PhD in Tarragona @ Universitat Rovira i Virgili.

I'm interested in collective dynamics. I keep coming back to how systems behave differently as interactions between their parts change. I've worked on diffusion and synchronization dynamics in networks, and more recently on how brain connectivity shapes its activity.

I'm now looking at learning dynamics in a similar way.
