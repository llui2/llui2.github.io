Boundary pixels oscillate around $\mathbf{r}_0$ at different scales $k$.

$$
\mathbf{r}(t) = \mathbf{r}_0 + \sum_k \underbrace{A_k\left[\mathbf{t}_k\sin\phi_k(t) + \epsilon\,\mathbf{t}_k^{\perp}\cos\phi_k(t)\right]}_{\Delta\mathbf{r}_k(t)}.
$$

$\mathbf{t}_k$ and $\mathbf{t}_k^{\perp}$ are the local unit tangent and normal. Their amplitudes are $A_k$ and $\epsilon A_k$. The quarter cycle phase difference gives an ellipse, made discrete by rounding to the grid.

The red pixel is $\mathbf{r}(t)$.
