$$
\phi_k(t) = \theta_k - \omega_k t.
$$

$\theta_k$ is the initial phase and $\omega_k$ the angular frequency. Phases come from the complex iterations and are averaged locally. Larger neighbourhoods follow broader contours.

The examples isolate broad, medium and fine scales, with enlarged movement.

On the main page, phases also vary slowly. Displacement is capped and rounded to the grid. Colour stays fixed.
